About IronMap

IronMap was created to solve a simple but common problem — finding the right gym can be overwhelming. With so many options available, beginners and experienced athletes alike often struggle to choose a gym that fits their goals, budget, and training style.

IronMap helps users discover gyms that align with their fitness goals, whether that means strength training, general fitness, or athletic performance.

Beyond gym discovery, IronMap focuses on guidance — helping users understand what kind of environment and workouts best support their long-term success.

Our mission is to remove guesswork, save time and money, and empower users to stay consistent on their fitness journey.

Built for beginners. Trusted by lifters. Designed for progress.
